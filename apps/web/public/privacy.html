Privacy policy

Your email, and nothing else.

Last updated 1 October 2026

The short version. If you join the waitlist, we keep your email address so we can tell you when Blocky opens to you. We don't use it for anything else, we never sell it, and you can have it deleted at any time by writing to blockywallet@gmail.com. This website has no cookies, no analytics and no trackers.

This policy covers this website and its waitlist. The Blocky app handles more than an email address, and its own privacy policy will be published here before it opens to the public.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we store:

  • your email address;
  • which form you used (the one at the top or the bottom of the page), so we know which works better;
  • the date and time you signed up, which is how we know your place in line.

That's all. We don't ask for your name, and we don't build a profile of you.

To stop people flooding the waitlist with fake signups, our server keeps the IP address of each signup attempt in its working memory for up to an hour. It is never written to a database or a file, and it disappears when the hour is up or the server restarts. Like any hosting company, our hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs of requests (such as IP address and time) to run and secure its service.

What we don't collect

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, ad networks or tracking pixels. Our font is served from our own server, so loading the page doesn't send your IP address to anyone else either.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • Your email is used for one thing: to tell you when Blocky is available to you, and to send you an invite. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by joining the waitlist and can withdraw at any time.
  • Short-lived IP addresses are used only to block abuse. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the waitlist free of spam (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We will never sell your email, share it with advertisers, or sign you up for anything else. If we ever want to send you something other than your invite, we'll ask first.

Who else handles it

We use two providers, each only to do the job described:

  • Railway (Railway Corporation, USA) hosts this website and the database the waitlist is stored in.
  • Google (Gmail) is what we use to send you your invite and to answer your emails.

These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, the transfer is protected by the safeguards GDPR requires, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

We keep your email until we've sent you your invite to Blocky, and delete it after that unless you've become a Blocky user. If you ask us to delete it sooner, we will. Either way, we won't keep a waitlist email for more than 24 months from the day you signed up.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you and give you a copy (access and portability);
  • correct it (rectification);
  • delete it (erasure) — this also takes you off the waitlist;
  • stop or limit using it (objection and restriction);
  • and you can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting anything done before.

Just email blockywallet@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. It's free, and we'll answer within one month.

If you think we've handled your data wrongly, you also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In the Czech Republic that is the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů), uoou.gov.cz. We'd appreciate the chance to fix it first.

Age

Blocky is for people aged 18 and over. Please don't join the waitlist if you're younger.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your email, we'll tell you by email before it takes effect.

Contact

For anything about your data, email blockywallet@gmail.com.
