Your keys, not ours

Your wallet belongs to you alone. Blocky can’t move, freeze or lose your money, and you can export your key and leave any time.

Your fingerprint, every time

Every payment is a card you read first and confirm with your fingerprint or face. The AI can’t approve anything.

The AI never builds the payment

The AI only says what you want. Fixed, open-source code builds the payment, and our server checks it before your phone will sign.

Addresses only from you

Blocky only sends to an address you typed or pasted yourself, a saved contact or an @username. Never to one it found or remembered.

Limits you set

Questions, answered.

Is Blocky a bank?

No. Blocky is software that helps you manage a wallet only you control. We never hold your money, so there’s nothing for us to freeze, lend out or lose.

Do I need to know anything about crypto?

No. Your balance is in dollars, and you talk to Blocky about money, not technology. Underneath, it runs on digital dollars (USDC) and open networks. That’s why sending is instant and global, and you never have to touch it.

Can the AI move my money on its own?

No. The AI can only propose. Every proposal is checked against your limits and shown to you as a card with the amount, the recipient and the fee. It only goes through when you approve it with your fingerprint or face.

What does the AI see, and who runs it?

Blocky’s assistant is Claude, made by Anthropic. It sees the messages you send it and what it looks up to answer you, like your balance or contacts. Your chat history is stored on your phone, not on our servers, and Anthropic doesn’t train its models on it. The full breakdown

What happens to my money if Blocky shuts down?

Nothing. Your money sits in your own wallet on a public network, not with us. Export your key from the app and open your wallet in another app like MetaMask.

Are the stocks real shares?

They’re tokenized stocks: tokens that track the price of a real share. They aren’t legal ownership of the share and don’t come with voting rights. Prices can go down as well as up, and nothing Blocky says is financial advice.

When can I get it?

Blocky is in a private beta on Android, and we’re letting people in from the waitlist in waves. iPhone is next. Join the list and we’ll email you when your spot opens.
