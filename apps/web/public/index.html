Questions

Is Blocky a bank?

No. Blocky is a non-custodial money app: your money sits in a wallet that only you control, not in an account we hold. That's also why nothing can move without your approval.

Do I need to know anything about crypto?

No. Your balance is in dollars, and you talk to Blocky about money, not technology. The crypto part is plumbing — it's why sends are instant and global — and you never have to touch it.

Can the AI move my money on its own?

No. Blocky can only propose. Every proposal is checked against your limits and shown to you as a card with the amount, the recipient and the fee. It only goes through when you approve it with your fingerprint, face or PIN.
